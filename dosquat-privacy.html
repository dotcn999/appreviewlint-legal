DoSquat 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 10 日

DoSquat 是一款深蹲训练与动作指导应用。我们高度重视您的隐私。

核心承诺：您的训练与健康相关数据保存在设备本地，我们不会将其上传到任何服务器（我们也不运营收集此类数据的服务器）。

1. 我们如何处理您的数据

DoSquat 采用「数据本地化」设计：深蹲计数、动作质量分析、训练计划、进度、成就与设置等数据均在您的设备上生成并存储。

2. 仅保存于设备本地的数据

  • 训练记录与深蹲计数
  • 动作质量 / 姿态分析结果
  • 训练计划、完成进度与成就
  • 应用设置与用户偏好
  • 高级版订阅解锁状态

这些数据保存在设备本地。卸载应用即可彻底删除全部本地数据，您也可在应用内清除相应数据。

3. 运动与传感器数据

应用使用设备运动传感器（仅需此一项权限）实时检测深蹲动作并计数、评估动作质量。该数据在设备上实时处理，不作为个人数据长期保存或上传。

4. 购买相关数据（唯一会离开设备的数据）

为提供内购（高级版月度 / 年度订阅、终身买断），我们使用 Apple StoreKit 与 RevenueCat。当您购买或恢复购买时，以下信息会经由 Apple / RevenueCat 用于校验与管理订阅：

  • RevenueCat 生成的随机匿名 App 用户 ID（不关联个人身份）
  • Apple 交易 / 收据信息与产品 ID
  • 订阅 / 购买状态

我们看不到您的支付卡信息——支付完全由 Apple 处理。

5. 我们不会做的事

  • 不要求注册账号，不收集邮箱 / 手机号
  • 不包含任何广告 SDK
  • 不进行跨应用追踪，不使用 IDFA
  • 不集成分析 / 埋点 / 崩溃上报 SDK
  • 不出售您的数据；除购买履约所需的 Apple / RevenueCat 外，不与第三方共享

6. 第三方服务

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：处理购买与支付。
  • RevenueCat：订阅管理与校验。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

7. 数据留存

本地数据保留至您删除为止。购买相关数据由 Apple / RevenueCat 按其各自政策留存，仅用于持续提供订阅服务。

8. 您的权利与选择

由于数据主要存于本地，您拥有完全控制权：卸载应用或在应用内清除数据即可删除。如需管理订阅，可在「系统设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中操作。就 Apple / RevenueCat 持有的少量数据，您可联系我们协助。

9. 儿童隐私

DoSquat 不面向 13 岁（或所在地区适用年龄）以下儿童，不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

10. 政策更新

本政策可能更新，更新后会在本页面发布并修改生效日期。

11. 联系我们

DoSquat Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

DoSquat is a squat training and form-coaching app. We take your privacy seriously.

Our core promise:Your workout and health-related data stays on your device. We do not upload it to any server, and we do not operate a server that collects this kind of data.

1. How We Handle Your Data

DoSquat is designed around on-device data: squat counts, form/quality analysis, training plans, progress, achievements, and settings are all generated and stored on your device.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

  • Workout history and squat counts
  • Form / movement quality analysis results
  • Training plans, completion progress, and achievements
  • App settings and user preferences
  • Premium subscription unlock status

This data lives locally on your device. Uninstalling the app fully deletes all local data; you can also clear relevant data from within the app.

3. Motion and Sensor Data

The app uses the device motion sensor (the only permission it requests) to detect and count squats and assess form in real time. This data is processed on-device and is not stored long-term or transmitted as personal data.

4. Purchase-Related Data (the only data that leaves your device)

To provide in-app purchases (Premium monthly / yearly subscriptions and a lifetime unlock), we use Apple StoreKit and RevenueCat. When you purchase or restore a purchase, the following is sent via Apple / RevenueCat to validate and manage your subscription:

  • A random, anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat (not tied to your identity)
  • Apple transaction / receipt information and product ID
  • Subscription / purchase status

We never see your payment card information — payment is handled entirely by Apple.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • No account registration; we do not collect email or phone number
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No cross-app tracking; no IDFA
  • No analytics / telemetry / crash-reporting SDKs
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties except Apple / RevenueCat as needed to fulfill purchases

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: handles purchases and payment.
  • RevenueCat: subscription management and validation. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

Local data is kept until you delete it. Purchase-related data is retained by Apple / RevenueCat according to their own policies, only for as long as needed to provide the subscription service.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because your data lives primarily on your device, you are fully in control: uninstall the app or clear data from within the app to delete it. To manage subscriptions, use System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. For the minimal data held by Apple / RevenueCat, contact us and we will assist.

9. Children's Privacy

DoSquat is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
